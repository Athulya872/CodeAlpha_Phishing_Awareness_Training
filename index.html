What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which attackers pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, or downloading harmful attachments.

📧 Email Phishing

Attackers send fake emails that imitate banks, companies, or other trusted organizations to steal personal information.

📱 Smishing

Smishing uses fraudulent SMS or text messages to trick users into clicking suspicious links or sharing sensitive information.

☎️ Vishing

Vishing uses fraudulent phone calls or voice messages to obtain passwords, OTPs, and banking details.

Common Warning Signs of Phishing

Recognizing these warning signs can help you avoid phishing attacks.

⚠️ Urgent Messages

Messages that threaten to close your account or demand immediate action.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Links with unfamiliar domains, misspellings, or misleading website addresses.

🔑 Personal Information Requests

Unexpected requests for passwords, OTPs, banking details, or confidential information.

📎 Unexpected Attachments

Unusual attachments that may contain malicious files or lead to unsafe downloads.

How to Protect Yourself

1. Think Before Clicking

Avoid clicking unexpected links or opening suspicious attachments.

2. Verify the Sender

Check the sender's address and confirm unexpected requests through official channels.

3. Protect Your Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords and never share your passwords or OTPs with others.

4. Enable MFA

Use multi-factor authentication to add an additional layer of account security.

5. Keep Software Updated

Update your operating system, browser, applications, and security software.

6. Report Phishing

Report suspicious emails and messages using your organization's or platform's reporting tools.
